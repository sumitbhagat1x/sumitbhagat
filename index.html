About Me

I'm Sumit Bhagat, a data professional interested in turning raw data into organized reports, meaningful insights, and practical business solutions.

My work focuses on Excel, Power BI, SQL, data analysis, and reporting. I enjoy building dashboards, improving reporting formats, organizing data, and finding practical ways to present information clearly.

I believe useful reporting should be accurate, easy to understand, and designed around the needs of the people using it. I'm continuously improving my technical and professional skills through practical projects and work experience.
